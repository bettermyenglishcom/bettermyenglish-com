FAQ ℹ️

What is this?

Better My English is a global online English As a Second Language (ESL) practice group.

How much does membership cost?

Group practice is FREE.

1:1 practice is SET YOUR OWN RATE (anything >$0 USD).

Who can join?

Anyone who wants to practice English can join!

How do I join?

For group practice, please join our Discord!

For 1:1 practice, please join our Discord server; message us on WhatsApp; or send us a DM on Instagram.

GET UPDATES 📃

Get reports about your English ability!

Group members receive general reports every quarter.

1:1 members receive reports every month.

TELL YOUR STORY 🎥

We want to show your progress and tell your story on YouTube‼️

You receive 100% of the money made by any video about you!

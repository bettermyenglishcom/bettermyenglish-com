RULES

⚖️

If we think you are using our services for ANYTHING criminal or unethical, we will ban you with NO warning.

Better My English is a safe space to practice English. We do not make exceptions.

By using Better My English, you agree to follow these Rules:

Practice English

We love all languages, but please only speak English here so everyone learns fastest!

Be Appropriate & Respectful

  • NO bullying, threatening, or harassment.
  • NO sexual comments, hate speech, violence, stalking, scams, etc.
  • NO asking for personal information (address, phone number, photos, money, passwords, accounts, etc.).
  • Follow Moderator instructions; report any Moderator breaking the Rules to the Host.
  • Respect everyone's time — stay on topic during group practice.

18+ Years Old Only

Anyone under 18 must have a parent/guardian present at all times.

Anyone who breaks this rule will be banned — no questions, no exceptions.

Do Not Record Without Permission

Better My English already records 1:1 practice sessions for you.

Do not record any group practice sessions without permission.

BetterMyEnglish.com will never collect your device data or use any cookies whatsoever. Please report any experience to the contrary.
